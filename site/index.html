CHIPFORGE
Channels Workflow Run locally Open the studio

Write 8-bit music in your browser.

Chipforge is a chiptune workstation with four sound-chip channels, a retro drum machine, a piano roll, a song chain and WAV export. It runs entirely in the page. Nothing to install, no account.

Open the studio ▶ npx chipforge

THE SOUND

Five channels, modeled on the chips

Every voice is synthesized live with the Web Audio API from the same building blocks the old consoles used. Volume envelopes can step in 4-bit increments at 60 frames a second, like the hardware.

Pulse

Leads and arps with the classic hollow-to-buzzy square sound.

DUTY 12.5 · 25 · 50 · 75%

Triangle

The stepped triangle behind every great console bassline.

32 STEPS · 4-BIT

Wave

Draw your own waveform with the mouse, or start from organ, saw or buzz.

32 SAMPLES · 16 LEVELS

Noise

A real linear-feedback shift register for hats, snares, risers and surf.

15-BIT LFSR · WHITE / METALLIC

Retro drums

Kick, snare, clap, hats, toms and crash, with tune, decay and crunch.

8 VOICES · BITCRUSH 3–16

THE WORKFLOW

Everything you need to finish a track

Sketch a loop in a minute, then arrange it into a song and render it out.

Piano roll and step grid

Draw, stretch and drag notes, with the other channels shown faintly behind. Drums get a step grid with accents and drag painting.

Scale-aware editing

Pick a key and scale and the piano roll lights up the notes that belong. Turn on snap and every note you draw lands in key. Ten scales, from major and minor to blues.

Selection and velocity

Shift+drag to select notes, then move, nudge, copy, duplicate or delete them. Shape dynamics in the velocity lane, or hit Humanize to loosen a stiff pattern.

Note effects

Give any note a slide, dive, rise, vibrato, staccato, ratchet or its own arpeggio (major, minor, dominant 7th), the way tracker music does.

Generators

Spread any drum hit evenly across the bar with Euclidean rhythms, or ask for a new melody in your key and scale, from sparse to busy.

Patterns and a song chain

Up to 16 patterns of 16, 32 or 64 steps. Drag them into order, and switch patterns live while looping.

Instrument controls

Envelope, glide, pitch sweep, delayed vibrato and a chord arpeggiator up to 60 Hz, with presets like Hero Lead, Coin and Laser.

Play and record

Play from the keyboard (Z–M, Q–U) or a MIDI controller. Record live while the song plays, or enter notes one step at a time.

Mixer and master

Volume, pan, mute, solo and meters on every channel. A tempo-synced echo, tone filter, compressor, swing and a metronome.

Two demo songs

Open Neon Cavern, a bright adventure theme, or Boss Rush, a 168 BPM harmonic-minor battle track, and take them apart.

Undo for everything

Edits, generators, imports and demo loads are all undoable, up to 150 steps back.

Share, save and export

Projects save themselves in your browser. Copy a link that opens your song anywhere, keep it as a .json file, or export a 44.1 kHz WAV of the mix or one WAV per channel.

OFFLINE

Run it on your own machine

The launcher is one small Node script with no dependencies. It serves the studio on localhost and opens your browser.

# from npm
$ npx chipforge

# from a clone of the repo
$ node cli.js --port 9000 --no-open
